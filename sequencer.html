Tracks

Click a step to edit it, double-click to turn it on or off, and drag a note's right edge to hold it longer. Keys: Space play/stop, ←→ move, ↑↓ transpose (⇧ octave), Enter add a note, ⌫ delete, Esc close the editor, 1–8 select a track. With the editor open and the sequencer stopped, notes played on your keyboard fill in the step, then move to the next one.
